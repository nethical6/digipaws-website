2. App Description

Digipaws is an Android application designed to help you manage monitor and reduce your screen usage. The App utilizes accessibility and draw-over-other-apps permissions to provide real-time usage statistics, such as usage time and reels count, directly on your screen when the usage tracker is enabled. The App is intended for users who want to take control of their screen time and improve their digital habits. The app also has functionalities to block various apps and addictive elements selected by user.

3. Permissions

3.1 Accessibility Permission: The App requires accessibility permission to monitor app usage and provide accurate statistics. This permission is used strictly for its stated purpose and not for any unintended or unauthorized activities.

3.2 Draw Over Other Apps Permission: This permission is required only when you enable the usage tracker. It is used to display real-time statistics, such as usage time and reels count, over other apps. This overlay functionality helps users stay aware of their screen usage without interrupting their workflow.

Important: All permissions are requested transparently and in accordance with Google Play's User Data policy. No permissions are used for unauthorized data collection or sharing.
